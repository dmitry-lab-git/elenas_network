Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started, training, and participation formats in Elena Melikhova's team.

How do I start collaborating with Elena Melikhova's team in Pavlodar? +

Simply message us on WhatsApp or Telegram for an initial introduction. We will discuss your goals—whether you are looking for additional income, starting your own business, or purchasing eco-products for personal use—and choose a comfortable starting format. Registration in the Greenway Global partner network is free with full mentor support.

Do I need to be located in Pavlodar to grow in the team? +

No, geography is not a limitation. Pavlodar hosts our Regional Center at 38 Yestay Street, live meetings, presentations, and Product Day events. At the same time, the entire training system, team briefings, and mentor support are structured online—partners from various cities across Kazakhstan, Russia, and Germany are already actively growing in our team.

Is prior experience in network marketing or sales required? +

No prior experience is required. Elena's Network provides a step-by-step onboarding system built on over 25 years of industry practice. New partners receive ready-to-use workflows, product training, and regular feedback at every stage.

What is the difference between a Greenway client and a business partner? +

The client format provides access to the Greenway Global eco-product catalog, personal product selection consultations, and our private Eco Market chat. Business partnership additionally unlocks team training, the company's compensation plan, and tools for building your own partner network.

How much time do I need to dedicate at the beginning? +

The schedule is flexible and depends on your goals. Most team members start with 1–2 hours a day, combining Greenway with their primary job, studies, or family commitments, and gradually scale up as their results grow.

Message the mentor directly, and we will discuss your personal starting plan.
